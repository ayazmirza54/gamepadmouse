Using it day to day

Switching apps

Hold Select and tap RB to step forward or LB to step back. Let go of Select to open the app you picked.

Typing

Press Y to open the on-screen keyboard and click its keys with Λ. Press Y again to close it.

Typing by voice

Click into a text box, press LB + RB and speak. Windows types what you say. Press them again to stop.

To use another dictation app, change the shortcut next to "LB + RB sends" in the app window.

Playing a game

Press Mode or Select + Start to pause GamepadMouse so the game gets the controller. The pad buzzes to confirm.

Dictating with OpenWhispr

Hold LT + RT while you speak, then let go. GamepadMouse holds Ctrl+Win for you, so it works with OpenWhispr's tap mode and its push-to-talk mode.

Changing a control

Open the app's Controls tab and double-click a row. Pick a mouse click, any keyboard shortcut, the on-screen keyboard, app switching or nothing. Shortcuts can tap once, stay held, or repeat.

Turbo and Clear

These work inside the gamepad, so GamepadMouse never sees them. If a button starts repeating by itself, turbo is set on it. Use Clear to remove it.

Gamepad basics

Power on

Press Mode. GamepadMouse picks the pad up within a second.

Sleep

The pad sleeps after 1 minute without a connection, or after 5 minutes with no buttons pressed. Press Mode to wake it.

Charging

Plug in the Type-C port on top. When connected, the active LED blinks slowly. When not connected, all 4 LEDs blink slowly. The blinking stops when it is fully charged.

Not responding?

Press the reset button in the small hole on the back of the pad, then press Mode to turn it on again.
